Subject: Project Falcon Acquisition Update

The board has approved moving forward with the acquisition of Northwind 
Analytics for an estimated valuation of $42 million. Due diligence is 
expected to conclude by end of Q3. Key stakeholders should not discuss 
this externally until the public announcement scheduled for October 15th.
The target company's core team of 12 engineers will be retained under 
a two-year vesting agreement.
