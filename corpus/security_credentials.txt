CONFIDENTIAL - SECURITY
Internal staging environment credentials and API keys must never be 
shared in plaintext outside approved secrets management tools. The 
production database connection string, third-party payment gateway 
keys, and internal admin panel access tokens are considered critical 
secrets requiring immediate rotation if ever exposed.

Production database passwords and administrator credentials are confidential secrets and must not be shared outside approved secrets management tools. Credentials for root and administrator accounts are sensitive authentication secrets, even when passwords use substituted letters, numbers, or symbols.
